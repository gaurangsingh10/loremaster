From **14 real finished episodes** in `output/usage_log.json` (gpt-6-luna; prompt caching on; full Skeptic). Generated by `python scripts/costs.py`.

| Length | Episodes measured | Avg input tok | Avg cached | Avg output tok | **Avg cost / episode** | Min–max |

| 20 min | 1 | 80,835 | 54,173 | 12,345 | **$0.0100** | $0.0100–$0.0100 |
| 40 min | 1 | 182,508 | 119,389 | 30,285 | **$0.0240** | $0.0240–$0.0240 |

**Where the money goes (all measured episodes):** storyteller 44%, skeptic 28%, researcher 19%, showrunner 6%, librarian 2%

## Per user and at scale (assumption: 20 × 20-min episodes per user per month, one per workday commute)

| Users | LLM only (browser voice) / month | + natural voice (OpenAI TTS, estimate) / month |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $0.20 | $5.60 |
| 100 | $19.92 | $559.92 |
| 10,000 | $1,992.38 | $55,992.38 |

- LLM cost per 20-min episode: **$0.0100**. TTS estimate per episode: $0.270 (~3,000 words ≈ 18K chars at ~$0.015/1K chars, an estimate since the course key has no TTS).
- The takeaway: the *thinking* is cheap and the *voice* is the expensive part. That's why the browser voice is the default and generated audio is cached on disk, so a replay costs $0.
- Guardrails cap the worst case: $0.05 per episode hard stop, 5 new episodes per 10 minutes per client.
