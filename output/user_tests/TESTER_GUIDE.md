# Loremaster tester guide (about 15 minutes)

Thanks for helping! Loremaster is an AI storyteller for your commute. It tells long, lecture-style stories about history and Warhammer 40K / Star Wars lore, and it grades every claim (Documented / Debated / Legend, or Canon / Legends / Fan theory) with sources.

## Commuters (SOM classmates)
1. Gaurang will either share his screen or sit you down at his laptop. The app runs locally at http://localhost:5173.
2. Pick **any thread** that sounds fun and **10 min**. Press ▶ and listen to chapter 1 like you would on a train. Glance at the screen whenever you like.
3. At the end of chapter 1, pick a branch. Listen to some of chapter 2.
4. Tap two or three of the little ✓ / ◆ chips in the text (**"Is that true?"**).
5. Try one of the new features: open the **Interview** tab and ask Julius Caesar (or anyone) one question, **or** press **📸 Snap & Story** and photograph something nearby (a coin, a book cover, a statue on your phone).
6. Open **Feedback**: rate it, how many minutes you listened, whether you'd listen tomorrow, your favorite moment, and one honest sentence we can quote (name and role optional).
7. Bonus (2 min): pick one claim and time how long it takes you to check it on Google, versus one tap in the app.

## Lore experts (40K / Star Wars fans)
1. Open the file **`blind_test_<thread>.html`** Gaurang sends you. It opens in any browser, offline.
2. Mark each of the 10 claims **True / False / Not sure / Fan theory**, from your own knowledge (no Googling).
3. Click **Download my answers** and send the `.json` file back.
4. If you can, also try one episode in the app and tell us whether the **canon grades** look right to you. Which claim was graded wrong?

What we measure: how long people listen, whether they'd come back, how much they trust the grades, and whether fans agree with them. Your feedback goes into a class project report. We only use the name or role you choose to give.
