The problem: great stories, no way to check them

  • Commuters who love history and deep lore (40K, Star Wars) listen to podcasts and lecture videos: fixed catalog, can't steer
  • ChatGPT will talk about anything, but legend and fact sound the same
  • So myths spread: "Texas divorce law comes from Muslim Spain." The sources say Visigothic and Castilian law; the Islamic link is Debated at best

Who

Me, SOM classmates on Metro-North and I-95, and the 40K and Star Wars fans I argue with.

Podcasts can't be steered or questioned; wikis can't be heard; chatbots sound equally sure about fact and legend.
