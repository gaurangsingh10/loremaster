The problem: great stories, no way to check them

  • Podcasts: brilliant, but you can't steer them or stop to ask
  • Chatbots: legend sounds exactly like fact
  • So myths spread: "Texas divorce law comes from Muslim Spain." The sources point to Visigothic and Castilian law. Debated at best

Who it's for

Commuters who love history and deep lore: me, SOM classmates on Metro-North and I-95, and the 40K and Star Wars fans I argue with.
