Galactic Senate (archive copy) was the legislative body of the Galactic Republic, in which senators represented member worlds and sectors. During the Separatist Crisis the Senate debated whether to create an army for the Republic, and it voted to grant Supreme Chancellor Palpatine emergency powers to respond to the crisis.

Senators met in the Senate Rotunda on Coruscant, where repulsorlift pods carried delegations to the center of the chamber. Many senators believed the emergency powers granted to Palpatine would be temporary and returned once the crisis ended.
